01The approach

Pradeep Realtors holds no inventory and pushes no project. As an authorised channel partner, we study the builder, the location, the price and the paperwork — then show you only what stands up to that check.

Zero cost to you

Our fee comes from the builder's marketing budget, never from your pocket. The price you pay through us is the same — often better, with channel-partner launch offers — than walking in directly.

You own it directly

Every rupee goes from you to the developer, against a registered agreement in your own name. We never collect or hold your money.

Guidance till the keys

Shortlisting, site visits, price negotiation, home-loan help, agreement review and registration follow-up — one person stays with you across the whole journey.
